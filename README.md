JumuiaX is an active project I'm building: a Kenyan local-services marketplace that connects customers with salons, barbers, spas, wellness professionals, and other service businesses.

The idea has grown beyond a simple backend system into a broader marketplace ecosystem. Customers can discover services and make bookings, while businesses can manage their services, branches, staff schedules, and appointments.

What I'm interested in is not just making the platform work, but understanding the engineering behind it — from application logic and data management to system architecture and the challenges of building software that can grow.

- 🛍️ **Marketplace:** Connecting customers with service providers
- 📅 **Bookings:** Making service discovery and appointment booking easier
- 🏢 **Business management:** Supporting services, branches, staff, and schedules
- 🏗️ **Engineering:** Learning through the real challenges of building and evolving a software platform
