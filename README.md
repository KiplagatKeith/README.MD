## 🚀 Active Project

### [JumuiaX — Discover. Book. Experience.](https://jumuiax.com)

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://jumuiax.com">
    <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Explore-JumuiaX-0D1117?style=for-the-badge&logo=googlechrome&logoColor=58A6FF" alt="Explore JumuiaX" />
  </a>
</p>

**JumuiaX is a Kenyan service marketplace connecting customers with salons, barbers, spas, and wellness professionals.** It also gives businesses tools to manage their operations and improve their visibility online.

The idea began with a simple problem: service providers need a better way to get discovered by customers. I'm building on that idea, developing the platform from its backend foundations toward a broader marketplace ecosystem.

**What the platform is designed to support:**

- 🔎 **Service discovery:** Help customers discover local businesses and the services they offer.
- 📅 **Booking management:** Make it easier for customers to book services and manage their appointments.
- 🏢 **Business management:** Support services, branches, staff schedules, and bookings.
- 🤝 **Professional visibility:** Help service providers reach more customers.
- 🏗️ **System evolution:** Progress from a single-business system toward a multi-tenant marketplace ecosystem.

JumuiaX is an active project I'm developing and growing. It reflects my interest in building practical software around real-world problems and understanding the engineering involved in taking an idea beyond its initial implementation.

🌐 **Live platform:** [jumuiax.com](https://jumuiax.com)  
📖 **About the project:** [Learn more about JumuiaX](https://jumuiax.com/about)
